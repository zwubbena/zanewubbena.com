π “forever”

Pi, written π, is the number you get when you divide a circle’s distance around the outside (the circumference) by its straight-line distance across the middle (the diameter).

π = circumference ÷ diameter

1. The Core Idea

The Magic Ratio: No matter how tiny a coin or how huge a wheel is, its circumference divided by its diameter always gives the same number.

The Short Value: In everyday math, people usually shorten pi to 3.14, or to the fraction 22/7.

The 3-Diameter Rule: If you take the diameter and wrap it around the edge of the circle, it fits 3 times, with a tiny slice of about 0.14 of a diameter left over.

2. Key Properties
