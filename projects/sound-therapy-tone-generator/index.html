Step 2. Speed of sound in dry air

°C

Speed of sound= 331.3× 1+temperature273.15
343.21 m/s= 331.3 m/s× 1+20273.15
  1. Divide the temperature by 273.15
    20273.15=0.07321984
  2. Add 1
    1+0.07321984=1.07321984
  3. Take the square root
    1.07321984=1.03596324
  4. Multiply by 331.3 meters per second
    331.3 m/s×1.03596324= 343.21 m/s

Temperature is the air temperature in °C. 273.15 is 0 °C on the kelvin scale. 331.3 meters per second is the speed of sound at 0 °C. Steps a to c keep eight decimals, and step d keeps two.
