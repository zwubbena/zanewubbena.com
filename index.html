About

Welcome! I'm Dr. Zane Wubbena, Managing Director of Forecasting in the Division of State Funding, Forecasting & Fiscal Analysis at the Texas Education Agency. I'm also an aviation enthusiast and pilot.

Projects

PilotDECODER: an aviation weather education tool.

Tools

  • d = rt Calculator: solves distance, rate, or time from the other two and shows each step, including unit conversions.
  • Percent Change Calculator: shows the formula with your values filled in, and how to phrase the result, including percentage points versus percent change.
  • Percent Difference Calculator: compares two values when neither one is the starting point, and shows when to use percent difference instead of percent change.
  • Tip Calculator: quick 20%, 15%, and 10% options, the total, an even split, and how to work out the tip in your head.
  • Time Zone Converter: converts between UTC (Zulu) and every U.S. time zone, step by step, with a map showing the same moment in each zone.
  • Decimal Hours Converter: converts hours and minutes to decimal hours and back, such as 1 hour 15 minutes to 1.25 hours.
